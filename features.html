Features & Threat Detection Capabilities

An exhaustive, auditable breakdown of every scam type, behavioral heuristic, and on-device NLP rule engineered into EdgeGuard to protect users in real time.

01

Core System Features & Architecture

100% On-Device AI / NLP Engine

Executes text classification, keyword vector matching, and NLP inference entirely on the mobile processor using a quantized TensorFlow Lite model (A0908i). Zero messages are sent to external servers.

Complex Event Processing (CEP) / UBA

Correlates asynchronous system events (unknown phone calls, SMS notifications, app switching, screen time, and temporal deltas) to detect multi-stage social engineering that static scanners miss.

Real-Time Floating Warning Overlays

Renders high-visibility floating warning cards directly over active messaging chats, phone dialers, or browsers using Android's WindowManager, giving instant explanations and intervention buttons.

Encrypted Local DB & 12-Day Auto-Purge

All threat detections and audit records are saved in a local, encrypted Room SQLite database on your device. To ensure total data hygiene, records older than 12 days are automatically deleted.

02

The 20 Scams Diagnosed & Alerted

Group A

Behavioral, Call & Social Engineering Scams

1. Digital Arrest Scam

High Severity

Mechanism: Scammers pose as Police/CBI/Narcotics on video calls, intimidating victims into keeping the call active and opening their bank app to transfer "security funds".

Detection: Active unknown video call > 15 minutes + Banking/UPI app opened during call or within 2 hours.

Alert: Digital Arrest Warning Overlay

2. OTP Theft / Voice Phishing (Vishing)

High Severity

Mechanism: Scammer calls claiming to be bank or telecom support and urges the victim to speak the OTP aloud while a fraudulent transaction is pending.

Detection: High-value OTP arrives while on call with an unknown caller, or unknown call arrives within 7 minutes of an OTP.

Alert: Full-Screen OTP Warning Overlay

3. Screen-Sharing / RAT Takeover Scam

Device Threat

Mechanism: Fraudsters instruct victims to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or RustDesk to solve a fake issue, granting full remote view of phone screens.

Detection: Package install of blacklisted remote software during or within 1 hour of a call with an unknown number.

Alert: RAT Watchdog Warning Overlay

4. Sextortion / Video Call Blackmail

Pre-Answer

Mechanism: Unknown contact initiates an unexpected video call to record the victim's face alongside explicit footage for extortion.

Detection: Incoming VoIP video call notification in WhatsApp, Telegram, Meet, Zoom, or Skype from an unsaved number.

Alert: Sextortion Warning Overlay
Group B

Malicious App, APK & Sideloading Scams

5. Pre-Install APK Shield

Threat: Trojan APKs sent on WhatsApp (Fake e-Challan APK, Wedding Card APK, PM-Kisan APK).

Detection: Detects Android Package Installer screen attempting to install unverified APKs outside Google Play.

Alert: Pre-Install Warning Card

6. Post-Install Sideload Watchdog

Threat: Background installs originating from web browsers, Telegram, or unofficial file managers.

Detection: Inspects getInstallSourceInfo() on package added events to flag unauthorized sources.

Alert: Post-Install Sideload Alert

7. Browser APK Download Interceptor

Threat: Drive-by APK downloads triggered by clicking malicious ad banners in Chrome or Samsung Internet.

Detection: Accessibility node inspection of browser download prompts matching .apk.

Alert: Malicious APK Download Alert
Group C

Social Funnels & Pre-Emptive Search Intent Scams

8. Meta / Instagram Ad Scam Funnel

Threat: Sponsored Instagram/Facebook trading ads promising 500% profits that funnel users directly into Telegram/WhatsApp scam groups.

Detection: Rapid app transition (< 60s) from Instagram, Facebook, or browser into group invite UI ("Join Group" / "Join Channel").

Alert: Scam Funnel Warning Overlay

9. Direct Message (DM) Funnel Jump

Threat: Social ads jumping users into 1-on-1 WhatsApp chats with scammers claiming to offer part-time jobs or crypto investments.

Detection: Immediate transition from ad/browser into an unsaved numeric contact conversation.

Alert: DM Scam Funnel Overlay

10. Fake Hotel & Tour Booking Scam

Threat: Fake hotel listings on search engines redirecting users to WhatsApp, where scammers demand QR code advance payments.

Detection: Browser hotel search + WhatsApp chat within 30 min + Payment/QR/UPI request text detected on screen.

Alert: Booking Scam Warning Overlay

11. Pre-Emptive Customer Care Scam

Threat: SEO-poisoned fake customer care and toll-free numbers placed on Google by cybercriminals.

Detection: Detects user typing customer care or helpline queries in browser URL/search bars.

Alert: Pre-Emptive Helpline Safety Alert

12. Pre-Emptive Instant Loan Trap

Threat: Predatory loan apps charging illegal fees and blackmailing victims using contact list access.

Detection: Browser search input matching instant loan queries.

Alert: Pre-Emptive Loan Safety Alert

13. Pre-Emptive Part-Time Job Scam

Threat: "Like YouTube videos for money" or "WFH data entry" requiring upfront security deposits or registration fees.

Detection: Browser search input matching online task and WFH job search patterns.

Alert: Pre-Emptive Job Safety Alert

14. Pre-Emptive Trading & Crypto Scam

Threat: Unregistered advisory groups promising guaranteed returns or risk-free crypto doubling.

Detection: Browser search queries matching stock tips, intraday signals, and crypto trading.

Alert: Pre-Emptive Trading Safety Alert
Group D

Message, Notification & Text Phishing Scams

15. Electricity Disconnection Scam

Detects messages claiming power will be cut tonight at 9:30 PM due to unpaid bills.

Engine: TFLite SLM + Deterministic

16. Bank / PAN / KYC Block Scam

Detects phishing notices claiming SBI YONO, bank accounts, or SIMs are suspended.

Engine: TFLite SLM + Vector Match

17. Fake Payment Credit (Reverse-UPI)

Detects forged credit SMS from personal numbers used to trick victims into refunding fake payments.

Engine: Sender Validation + Balance Heuristic

18. Fake Lottery / KBC Prize

Flags unsolicited SMS claiming ₹25 Lakh lottery victories in lucky draws.

Engine: TFLite SLM Classifier

19. Fake Virus Browser Spam

Intercepts rogue web notifications ("virus detected", "clean storage now", "mcafee expired").

Engine: Notification Listener Spam Rule

20. Obfuscated Betting Funnels

Detects illegal betting & Aviator links disguised with character spaces or leet-speak.

Engine: De-Leetification Engine

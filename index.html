Every Scam EdgeGuard Diagnoses & Neutralizes

Scammers constantly adapt their tricks. EdgeGuard provides 100% on-device, multi-modal defense against all 20 active social engineering, phone call, APK, and phishing scam vectors.

Category 01 4 Scams

Behavioral, Call & Social Engineering Defense

1. Digital Arrest Scam

High Severity

Threat Mechanism: Scammers pose as Police, CBI, ED, or Customs on video calls, keeping victims under psychological intimidation while instructing them to open banking apps to transfer "security deposits."

Detection Heuristic: Unknown video call lasting > 15 minutes + Banking/UPI app opened during call or within 2 hours.

🛡️ Alert: Digital Arrest Warning Overlay advising immediate disconnect.

2. OTP Theft / Voice Phishing (Vishing)

Temporal Guard

Threat Mechanism: Fraudster calls pretending to be bank/telecom support, initiates an unprompted transaction, and tricks the user into speaking the newly received OTP aloud over the call.

Detection Heuristic: High-value OTP arrives during an active unknown call, OR unknown caller dials within 7 minutes of an OTP, OR OTP arrives within 1 hour after call.

🛡️ Alert: Full-Screen OTP Warning Overlay: "NEVER share OTP over call!"

3. Screen-Sharing / RAT Takeover Scam

Hijack Shield

Threat Mechanism: Scammers instruct victims over phone calls to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or RustDesk to solve fake customer issues, gaining full remote visibility of phone screens and passwords.

Detection Heuristic: Package install of blacklisted remote control software during or within 1 hour of a phone call with an unknown number.

🛡️ Alert: RAT Watchdog Overlay urging immediate uninstall and hanging up.

4. Sextortion Video Call Blackmail

Pre-Answer

Threat Mechanism: Unknown contacts place unsolicited video calls. Once answered, prerecorded explicit videos are played while recording the victim's face to extort ransom.

Detection Heuristic: Incoming VoIP video call notification in WhatsApp, Telegram, Meet, Zoom, or Skype from an unsaved number.

🛡️ Alert: Pre-Answer Warning Overlay: "Unknown video call. Keep camera covered."

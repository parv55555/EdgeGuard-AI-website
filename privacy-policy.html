Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026 • Version 1.1 • Application: com.parv.safetexts

Our Fundamental Privacy Commitment

EdgeGuard operates on a strict 100% On-Device Zero-Egress Architecture. Unlike traditional security applications that transmit your messages, contacts, and browsing habits to cloud databases for remote analysis, EdgeGuard executes all neural classification, token matching, and behavioral heuristics locally on your mobile processor.

✓ 0 Bytes Cloud Egress
✓ No Remote Accounts
✓ No Ad Trackers

01. Information We Process and How It Is Used

EdgeGuard accesses specific device telemetry strictly to identify and neutralize cyber fraud in flight. All processing occurs in volatile system memory (RAM) and is never transmitted off your device.

02. Android Permissions & Justifications

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and Android Security Guidelines, the following table details every sensitive permission requested, its exact purpose, and its privacy isolation boundary:

Android Accessibility Service (AccessibilityService)
Essential Core

Why It Is Needed: Inspects active on-screen view node hierarchies across messaging applications (WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS), mobile web browsers (Chrome, Samsung Internet), and social media platforms (Instagram, Facebook).

What It Detects: Identifies deceptive stock trading ad funnels, uncatalogued phishing URLs, Trojan `.apk` download triggers (such as fake traffic e-challans or wedding card invitations), and cyber extortion patterns.

Privacy Guarantee: Does not monitor or record passwords, banking PINs, or keyboard keystrokes. Inspected text is evaluated immediately by on-device ML and instantly discarded.

Phone State & Call Monitoring (READ_PHONE_STATE & READ_CALL_LOG)
Behavioral UBA

Why It Is Needed: Detects incoming unknown cellular or VoIP calls that occur within 7 minutes of receiving a bank transaction OTP (one-time password).

What It Detects: Intercepts social engineering bank manager impersonation scams before users can read aloud their sensitive verification codes. Also checks for unknown incoming video calls associated with sextortion blackmail traps.

Privacy Guarantee: Caller IDs are compared strictly against your local device address book (`ContactUtils`). Call audio is NEVER recorded, listened to, or transmitted.

Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)
Real-Time Intervention

Why It Is Needed: Renders real-time floating scam alert cards directly over active messaging chats, ringing telephony screens, or browsers without closing your current window.

Privacy Guarantee: Overlays only display high-visibility warnings with explanatory indicators and dismissal buttons.

Package Installation Monitor (ACTION_PACKAGE_ADDED)
RAT Watchdog

Why It Is Needed: Alerts the user when a remote access software package (such as AnyDesk, TeamViewer, RustDesk, or AirDroid) is installed during or immediately after a call with an unknown caller.

Privacy Guarantee: Does not monitor app usage statistics outside checking for known remote administration tools.

03. What We Do NOT Collect

To maintain absolute privacy, EdgeGuard NEVER collects:

  • Your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
  • The contents of your personal private chat messages.
  • Account passwords, debit/credit card credentials, or bank login details.
  • Device location, GPS coordinates, or IP address tracking logs.
  • Advertising identifiers (GAID / AAID) or browsing histories.

04. Local Data Storage & 14-Day Purge Lifecycle

When a threat is intercepted, a cryptographic hash and scam indicator record are logged locally inside an encrypted SQLite Room database on your physical device storage (`/data/data/com.parv.safetexts/`).

This local history is managed by an automated 14-day record purging lifecycle. Records older than 14 days are permanently pruned by background tasks. You can also manually flush all cached logs at any time from within the application settings.

05. Third-Party Services and Cloud SDKs

EdgeGuard contains zero third-party analytical trackers, advertising SDKs, or data brokers. We do not integrate Google Firebase Analytics, Facebook Graph SDK, Flurry, AppsFlyer, or any external telemetry endpoints.

06. User Rights, Revocation & Uninstallation

You retain total sovereignty over your mobile device at all times:

  • Permission Revocation: You may toggle off Accessibility, Phone State, or Overlay permissions at any time via Android System Settings.
  • Complete Data Erasure: Uninstalling EdgeGuard immediately deletes the local encrypted Room database and all temporary neural memory caches. Zero residual traces remain.

07. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

EdgeGuard is an on-device security tool designed for general audiences. Because our application does not collect, transmit, or store personal information in the cloud, we do not knowingly harvest information from children under the age of 13.

The Genesis: Indore Cyber Cell to EdgeGuard AI

During the SafeClicks 2.0 Hackathon organized by the Indore Police, I analyzed live cyber fraud cases. The critical bottleneck: cloud-based security tools suffer from network round-trip latency, require sending sensitive personal messages off-device, and completely fail when victims are coerced into offline calls or hasty APK installs.

I engineered EdgeGuard AI to run 100% offline on the device itself. Combining <0.1ms deterministic heuristic reflexes with an INT8-quantized Small Language Model, it neutralizes scams right at the OS edge before unauthorized code can execute — with zero data leaving the phone.
